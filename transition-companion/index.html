For service members, veterans, and family members helping them. The steps are reminders to read official pages and to use the other tools in this folder. They are not a separation order, a benefits decision, or a TAP completion certificate.

You check each box yourself. The page does not check a box, open a form, or contact an agency because a date is near. Highlighting a window is only a visual cue. Checked boxes and the date stay in this browser under ovpt-companion-v1 until you clear them or download a backup. There is no network call from this checklist.

What is stored

Checked boxes and the date live in this browser’s localStorage under a key used only by this page. Download a backup if you want a copy you can move. There is no account. Links to VA, DoD, DOL, BLS partners, SBA, FTC, and IC3 open only when you choose them.

DoD COOL is linked at its public address. This checklist does not copy COOL catalogs. Open the official page yourself for credential information.
