What this file contains

Military codes and O*NET-SOC links are from the Defense Manpower Data Center crosswalk distributed by the O*NET Resource Center. Occupation descriptions, skills, sample titles, and job-zone text are from the O*NET 31.0 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, used under CC BY 4.0. This toolkit shortened the database to those fields and dropped rows that were not needed for this lookup. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications. O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA.

Handbook links are from the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook crosswalk. Resume phrases, curated civilian titles, jargon, acronyms, and rank wording are from Debriefed by Chris Simser, MIT License, commit 7d75063a4d816ab615cdfbe8868045490837f725.

Some official links point at military O*NET occupations, especially infantry codes in group 55. Those records describe the service job. Dictionary titles beside them are suggestions for a human to review. Additional skill identifiers, prefixes, Navy AQDs, and similar add-on codes are not in this offline file. Primary enlisted, warrant, and officer codes for the six branches are.
