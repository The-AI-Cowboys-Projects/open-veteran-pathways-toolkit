# Veteran usability pilot plan

**Built by The AI Cowboys.** The AI Cowboys Foundation, [www.theaicowboysfoundation.org](https://www.theaicowboysfoundation.org), a veteran-owned AI lab in San Antonio.

This is a plan. It is not a report of a pilot that already happened. No veteran service organization, university veteran center, or other group has agreed to take part by being named here. No user count, completion rate, or partner is claimed.

The Foundation is not affiliated with the Department of Defense or the Department of Veterans Affairs. A session under this plan is not a benefits appointment, a medical visit, or legal help. Outputs of the tools remain for human review.

## Purpose

Watch a small number of veterans, military family members, and older adults use the four tools. Learn where the wording, the contrast, the focus order, or a step fails. Change the pages from those notes. Publish the change in this repository. Do not publish names.

## Who to invite

Recruitment is an invitation, not a claim that anyone has accepted.

- Ask veteran service organizations, through a post or chapter service officer, to share an invitation with members who want to try the tools.
- Ask university veteran centers to post the same invitation for student veterans.
- Do not recruit from a benefits claim line, a medical waiting room, or a class roll. Do not offer the session as help with a claim.

If a thank-you is offered, decide that before the first invitation and write it in the consent note. This plan does not set an amount and does not say that funds are already committed.

Contact for the invitation and for questions: contact_us@theaicowboys.com. The public pages also have a Send feedback link to that address. The message asks people not to include personal information.

## Consent and no personal data

Before a session, the person gets a short written note they can keep:

- What they will be asked to do, and that they can stop at any time without giving a reason.
- That the Foundation is not DoD or VA, and that the session does not file, decide, or advise on a benefit.
- That notes describe the task and the obstacle. Notes do not include the person’s name, contact details, claim numbers, medical details, or a real message from their own life.
- That they should not type classified information, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), protected health information (PHI), or sensitive personally identifiable information (SPII). If they start to, the facilitator stops that task.
- That the synthetic messages in `eval/fixtures.json` are available if they want something to paste. Those messages are invented.

Consent forms, if a signature is collected, stay in a file that does not also hold the task notes. Task notes use a session number. There is no account in the toolkit and this plan does not add one. There is no third-party analytics tag on the pages. Do not add one for the pilot. Do not record the screen if the recording could capture a real document. If the person agrees to a recording of the empty tools only, say that in the consent note before you start.

## Tasks

Give the tasks in the same order. Read the safety line on the page with the person before the first task on that tool. Time each task only if a clock is started in the open. Do not invent a time after the fact.

**Career Pathway Translator.** Look up Army `11B` and Navy `HM` from the codes on the page. Paste this invented line and say whether the wording is usable: “I briefed flag officers and supervised personnel.” Do not paste a real evaluation.

**Scam Guardian.** Paste two fixtures from `eval/fixtures.json`: `va-suspend-gift` and `clinic-appointment`. Ask what the person would do next. Do not paste a real message.

**Plain-English AI Tutor.** Open the first lesson, answer one quiz question, and search the glossary for “phishing”. The model download is optional. Do not ask anyone to download it unless they ask and have read the size and the terms on the page.

**Transition Companion.** Check one step, enter a made-up date that is not their own, and find the backup button. Then clear the checklist so the browser does not keep the practice date. Do not enter a real separation date.

One task, if the person agrees, is done with the keyboard only: tab to the control, complete it, and hear or see the result. Skip that task if they do not want it. The skip link and the result heading are the things to watch.

## What to write down

These are the measures to collect. This file has no values for them.

- Task finished without a hint from the facilitator: yes or no, per tool.
- Minutes on the task, if a clock was used.
- Words they asked about.
- Whether they noticed the disclaimer before typing.
- Whether they moved toward typing real personal data. If they did, the task stops and the note says only that it happened, not what they typed.
- For the keyboard task: whether they reached the result without a pointer.
- Any contrast, text size, or focus problem they name.
- A 1 to 5 ease rating may be asked after each tool. Do not average those ratings into a public claim until a later note says how many people answered and how the question was worded. This plan does not contain that note.

Do not collect age, rank, disability status, or contact information in the task notes. If a follow-up is needed, keep that address with the consent file, not with the task notes.

## Sequence

This order has not been started by the act of writing it down.

1. Freeze the task script and use the synthetic messages already in the repository. Do not add real examples.
2. Send invitations to veteran service organizations and university veteran centers. Wait for a reply. Do not list a group as a participant until someone there agrees in writing to share the invitation or to host a session.
3. Sit with a first handful of people who volunteered. The plan does not set a quota. Revise wording, labels, or focus from the notes.
4. Run a second round only when the same obstacle shows up more than once and a change was made.
5. Describe the change in the repository. Do not publish names, session recordings, or raw notes.

## What this pilot will not claim

- That a benefit, a job, or a medical outcome changed.
- That the Department of Defense or the Department of Veterans Affairs sponsored, hosted, or reviewed the session.
- A count of public users of the website.
- That a named organization is a partner, unless a later commit quotes an agreement that organization actually made.
