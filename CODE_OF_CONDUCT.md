# Code of conduct

**Built by The AI Cowboys.** The Open Veteran Pathways Toolkit is a public project of The AI Cowboys Foundation, [www.theaicowboysfoundation.org](https://www.theaicowboysfoundation.org), a veteran-owned AI lab in San Antonio. People who contribute, review, or ask questions are expected to keep the work usable by veterans, military families, and older adults.

## Expected

- Be direct and respectful in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Criticize the change, not the person.
- Keep examples unclassified. Do not post someone else’s personal, medical, or financial information.
- Say what you can show. Do not add user counts, partnerships, or outcomes that the change does not demonstrate.
- Leave third-party licenses and the safety disclaimer intact.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, slurs, or sexual attention toward anyone.
- Publishing private information about another person.
- Suggestions that the tools should hide data collection or take action without the person at the keyboard.

## Reports

Report conduct problems through a GitHub issue that does not include personal data, or by email to contact_us@theaicowboys.com. Maintainers may edit or lock a conversation that breaks this policy. This document is the project’s own short policy.
