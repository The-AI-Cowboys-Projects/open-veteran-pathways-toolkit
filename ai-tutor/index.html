The lessons and glossary entries were written for the Open Veteran Pathways Toolkit by The AI Cowboys Foundation. They are original teaching text, not a copy of a commercial course and not an official DoD or VA curriculum. Definitions are plain-language explanations for veterans, military families, and older adults. They are not technical standards.
