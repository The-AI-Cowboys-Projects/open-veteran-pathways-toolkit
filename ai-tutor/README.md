# Plain-English AI Tutor

**Built by The AI Cowboys.** The AI Cowboys Foundation, [www.theaicowboysfoundation.org](https://www.theaicowboysfoundation.org), a veteran-owned AI lab in San Antonio.

Twelve lessons, 95 glossary entries, and 64 quiz questions, written for this toolkit. Every factual claim links a public source such as NIST, the FTC, VA, the Department of Labor, CISA, or a paper. The lessons do not invent statistics. Progress is stored in this browser only and can be downloaded as JSON. The lessons are not a certificate and not a government course.

Scam Guardian does not detect deepfakes. The synthetic-media lesson says that plainly and does not describe how to make one.

Open `index.html`. Search the glossary or start at lesson 1.
